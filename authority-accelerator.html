The eight things people ask before applying.

How long is Authority Accelerator?
4 months. Four pillars. You can stay in the community after the 4 months end.
Who is this for?
Heart-led practitioners, coaches, and service providers with a real gift. Healers, somatic practitioners, fertility specialists, copywriters, designers, consultants. Women who already have at least one paying client and are ready to build the system.
What if I have a small audience?
The system works at any audience size. Kristy hit her first $10K month with under 2,000 followers. Jennah closed $4,000 with under 1,000 followers. Small audiences close fast when the offer and the messaging are dialed in.
How much does it cost?
Pricing is shared on the call. The program is application-based. Apply through the form on this page and we'll send the qualifying questionnaire before the call.
Do I need to be on Instagram already?
Yes. The program runs through Instagram as the primary channel. If you're posting and not converting, that's the exact problem this solves.
What's the weekly time commitment?
About 10 hours a week. Built for women with limited time. Kristy ran the system as a mom with a server job. The Claude content engine cuts content creation to about 5 minutes a post.
Do I need to know AI tools already?
No. We teach the entire stack inside the program. Claude for content, ManyChat for the DM funnel, Canva for carousels.
What happens on the call?
It's a qualifying conversation. We learn about your business, your gift, and where you're stuck. If Authority Accelerator is the right fit we share pricing and next steps. If it isn't, we point you somewhere better.
